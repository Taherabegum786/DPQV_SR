Phase 1 of `docs/08-execution-plan.md`. **This file is the real problem source.**
A stated limitation in a published paper is a pre-validated gap with a citation
attached — worth more than any generated idea.

**Target: ≥40 rows from ≥25 papers by week 4.**

Rules:
- One row per stated limitation. Quote or paraphrase closely — do not editorialise.
- Source limitations, threats-to-validity, and future-work sections specifically.
- After adding a paper, do the **forward citation walk** and log who already
  addressed each limitation.
- Do not put your own ideas here. Those go in `scratch-ideas.md` and are not
  acted on until Phase 3.
